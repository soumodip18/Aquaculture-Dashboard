# 🌊 AQUABAS — India Aquaculture & Water Intelligence Dashboards

**AQUABAS** is an integrated aquaculture and water intelligence platform developed for **Mul Biotech Farms**.

The project brings multiple Power BI dashboards together through a single professional web interface, providing a unified view of aquaculture production, species intelligence, water resources, water quality, risk, sustainability, and related intelligence.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

AQUABAS is designed to support data-driven understanding of India's aquaculture ecosystem by combining:

- 🐟 Aquaculture production and species intelligence
- 🌊 Inland aquaculture intelligence
- 💧 Water-resource and water-quality monitoring
- ⚠️ Sustainability and risk indicators
- 📊 Power BI analytical dashboards
- 🗂️ Research and data-model documentation

The website acts as a central access point for the project's **four Power BI reports**.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Modules

### 1. 🐟 Production & Species

Provides insights related to:

- Aquaculture production
- Species intelligence
- Production trends
- Species-level analysis

### 2. 🌊 Inland Intelligence

Focuses on:

- Inland aquaculture
- Water-resource intelligence
- Inland ecosystem analysis
- Regional water information

### 3. 🌱 Sustainability & Risk

Provides insights into:

- Sustainability indicators
- Environmental risk
- Aquaculture-related risks
- Long-term resource management

### 4. 💧 Water Intelligence

Focuses on:

- Water resources
- Water-quality monitoring
- Water-quality assessment
- Risk identification
- Monitoring performance
